Wan 3.0 video generator: Standard vs Prime and every input mode

Wan 3.0 brings a serious new challenger to VeilPix video generation. Choose the lower-cost Standard mode or faster Prime mode, then direct a clip with text, exact frames, images, videos, audio, a document, or a public webpage.

The short answer

  • Choose Standard when cost matters most. It includes the complete Wan 3.0 workflow set.
  • Choose Prime when faster generation is worth the higher credit cost.
  • Start with 480P and five seconds when testing a prompt or reference setup, then increase quality after the motion works.
  • Use adaptive aspect ratio when you want the model to infer framing, or lock a ratio for a specific platform.

Four ways to direct a Wan 3.0 video

1. Text and multimodal references

Leave the reference area empty for text-to-video, or add up to 10 images, 5 videos, and 5 audio files. Name images in the prompt as Image1, Image2, and so on. Reference video and audio are limited to 15 seconds each, and reference video plus the requested output must fit inside the 30-second window.

2. Exact start and end frames

Start/End mode is designed for controlled animation. A start frame is required; the end frame is optional. Use it when the first composition must match exactly, when a shot needs to arrive at a specific visual destination, or when continuing a scene from another clip.

3. Document context

File mode accepts common document formats, including PDF, Word, spreadsheet, presentation, text, Markdown, and Apple productivity files. This is useful for grounding a visual concept in a brief, storyboard, product sheet, or written scene description.

4. Public webpage context

Link mode gives Wan 3.0 a public webpage that is available without signing in. Use it for a public product page, article, visual reference, or other accessible source. Private pages and local files should use their matching upload mode instead.

Resolution, duration, and aspect ratio

  • Resolution: 480P, 720P, or 1080P.
  • Duration: 2-30 seconds for normal workflows; video-reference clips can use a one-second minimum.
  • Aspect ratios: adaptive, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, or 9:16.
  • Audio: generate audio with the clip or turn it off.
  • Seed: reuse a non-negative integer when you want more reproducible iteration.

A practical first test

  1. Select Wan 3.0 Standard.
  2. Choose 480P and 5 seconds.
  3. Add one or two clear references instead of filling every slot.
  4. Describe the subject, action, camera movement, environment, and ending in that order.
  5. Move to Prime or a higher resolution only after the movement and composition are working.

Reference prompting tips

  • Give each reference one job: character identity, location, wardrobe, motion, sound, or visual style.
  • Use explicit motion verbs and a clear camera instruction such as tracking, orbiting, dollying, or holding steady.
  • Describe the intended final beat so the clip has somewhere to arrive.
  • For busy action, describe the order of events instead of stacking every action into one sentence.
  • Keep critical constraints near the beginning of the prompt and refer to numbered images consistently.

Private delivery and browser recovery

Wan 3.0 references upload directly from the browser to private temporary storage rather than being kept on the VeilPix application server. If the browser closes during generation, the server-side job continues. When the user returns within 48 hours, VeilPix can retrieve the finished result, save the real video blob in the browser-local Album, verify it, and then delete the temporary delivery copy.

The local Album is specific to that browser profile and is not an account-synced cloud gallery. Provider retention policies still apply to media processed by the selected model service.

Frequently asked questions

Does Prime have more features than Standard?

No. In VeilPix, both modes expose the same inputs, resolutions, aspect ratios, audio controls, and duration range. Prime is the faster, higher-cost option.

Can Wan 3.0 use a reference video?

Yes. Add up to five MP4 or MOV references, with no more than 15 seconds of reference video in total. The reference duration plus generated output must fit within 30 seconds.

Can I drag references from my VeilPix Album?

Yes. Images and videos stored in the browser-local Album can be dragged into compatible reference selectors, just like files imported from the device.
